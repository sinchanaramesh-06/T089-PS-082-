# Business-Rule & Edge-Case Traceability Register — Week 3

This register provides complete end-to-end traceability across the 17 business rules (BR-01–BR-17) and 8 explicit edge cases (EC-01–EC-08) defined in the Capstone Brief.

|---|---|---|---|---|

| **BR-06** | Vintage & Expiry | RECs expire 36 months after the end of the issuance month (month-end arithmetic). Expired certificates cannot be traded or retired. | **BR-11** | Buy-Order Margin | Block 100% of order value at entry (`quantity * limit_price`). | **BR-12** | FIFO Certificate Transfer | Sells consume oldest vintage first (`vintage`, `issue_date`, `lot_id`). | **BR-13** | RPO Targets & Penalties | Solar target 10%, non-solar 15% of annual consumption MWh. Retirements before 31 March count toward compliance year. | **BR-14** | RPO Carry-Forward | Entity retiring at least 90% of total target may carry remaining shortfall to next compliance year once (status `CARRY_FORWARD`, zero penalty). | **BR-15** | Reversal Handling | Verification failure reversal: cancel generator untraded units first, then buffer pool units. Deficit becomes monetary liability at last clearing price of category. | **BR-16** | Order Validation & Cancellation | Sell quantity cannot exceed unencumbered holdings of same category. Partial sells allowed. Orders cannot be modified, only cancelled. ## 2. Explicit Edge-Cases Traceability (EC-01 to EC-08)

| Edge Case ID | Edge Case Description | Planted Scenario / Context | Expected Outcome | Verification Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **EC-01** | Cumulative buy equals cumulative sell at two different prices | SES-01: Buy 1000 = Sell 1000 at all prices from 1800 to 2200 | Uniform clearing price chosen is lowest price: **Rs 1,800.00** | `test_ec_01_clearing_lower_price_on_tie` |
| **EC-02** | Marginal pro-rata leaving 1 residual unit after floor division | SES-02: 151 marginal demand vs 100 supply. Pro-rata floor division gives 33, 33, 33 = 99 | Exactly **1 residual unit** left unfilled; reported in `session_summary.csv` | `test_ec_02_marginal_prorata_one_residual_unit` |
| **EC-03** | March generation applied 6 months later on last day | ISS-029 (applied 2025-09-30) vs ISS-030 (applied 2025-10-01) | ISS-029 **ISSUED** (1240 units); ISS-030 **LAPSED** (`APPLICATION_AFTER_2025-09-30`) | `test_ec_03_march_generation_applied_six_months_last_day` |
| **EC-04** | Solar REC retirement used for non-solar shortfall before solar target met | ENT-04 solar target = 200, solar retired = 0; RET-003 requests substitution | **REJECTED** with reason `SOLAR_TARGET_NOT_MET`; allowed only after solar target satisfied | `test_ec_04_solar_retirement_for_non_solar_rejected_before_solar_target` |
| **EC-05** | Entity retiring exactly 90 percent | ENT-03 target = 1000. Case A: 900 retired (90.0%). Case B: 899 retired (89.9%) | Case A: **CARRY_FORWARD** (shortfall 100, penalty 0). Case B: **SHORTFALL** (penalty Rs 303,000) | `test_ec_05_entity_retiring_exactly_ninety_percent_carries_forward` |
| **EC-06** | Reversal exceeding untraded holdings plus buffer | ISS-008: 100 untraded, 0 buffer, 900 traded. Reversal of 1000 | 100 untraded cancelled; **Rs 1,620,000.00 liability** debited at last price; buyer units preserved | `test_ec_06_reversal_exceeding_untraded_plus_buffer` |
| **EC-07** | Buy order margin equals available funds, fee makes account negative | Available funds = Rs 116,000. Order 58 @ 2000. Settle with fee Rs 684.40 | Margin accepted; available becomes 0; settlement debits fee to **Rs -684.40**, blocking further buys | `test_ec_07_buy_order_margin_equals_available_fee_makes_account_negative` |
| **EC-08** | Trade matched on certificate expiry date | SES-03 on 2025-12-31 matches HLD-019 expiring 2025-12-31 | Trade is **VALID**; lot transferred; not expired until next day (2026-01-01) | `test_ec_08_trade_matched_on_expiry_date_is_valid` |
